About the Book

A boy stands at the rim of a deep well, thirsty, with no rope and no bucket. A woman stands over another well with the same problem, and a Stranger sitting on its rim asks her for a drink. Between those two wells lies the whole story of religion without acquaintance: baptized, attending, serving, and unsettled all the way, carried for years on the saddle of a Rider we can name but have never met.


A Genuine Fellowship is about coming out of the trees. Written with a builder’s eye and a shepherd’s patience, it walks seven movements — the ground, the Cornerstone, the radius, the plumb line, the load, the joints, and the city — from the first honest question to a life governed from the Center.

Along the way it faces the questions believers actually carry: prayers that seem unanswered, fires we did not light, quarrels among the servants of God, love and its counterfeits, work, doubt, and the long walk back.


No mask survives the journey. No reader is left outside it. The cross is the radius. The Center holds. And the door of this fellowship carries one sentence: “him that cometh to me I will in no wise cast out.”

About the Author

AUTHOR PHOTO

(add your photograph here)

Kibrom Gero Gebre was born in Bonga, in the green highlands of Kaffa, Ethiopia, the region where coffee was born. A civil engineer by profession, he has spent his working life among soils, foundations, plumb lines, and load calculations, which is why his writing keeps reaching for a builder’s tools whenever it needs to tell the truth about a soul.


He met Jesus Christ at thirteen, on a night of calling he has kept for another book, and has walked with Him since — a journey he sums up in two words: mercy and goodness. He serves among the people of God in congregation and in youth fellowship, in the word and in prayer.


A Genuine Fellowship: The Cross Radius is his first book. More is being written.
